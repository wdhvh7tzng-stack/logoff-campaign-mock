The internet has an answer for everything.
That doesn’t mean
it’s a good one.

From dating advice, to confidence talk, to what it means to be a man, teen boys are turning to search engines, influencers, and social media for answers. But algorithms and influencers can make harmful advice seem normal, leaving boys unsure of who to trust. But why?

Teen boys turn to online advice because it feels safer to ask a screen rather than someone they know. Little do they know they are not the only ones searching for the same answers.
